# Conversation vocabulary

40 signs for basic dialogue. **9 already exist as INCLUDE classes** (the live model can recognize them) and **31 must be recorded**. Generated by `tools/conversation_report.py` from `backend/config/conversation_vocabulary.json`, the model's `labels.json` and the clips on disk. No sign here is signer reviewed.

## What to record

- Signs to record: **31**.
- Takes: the guided session default is 8 takes per sign per signer, so **31 x 8 = 248 accepted takes per signer**.
- For the plan in `docs/TRAINING_PLAN_SELF_RECORDED.md` (signer roles S01 to S05, 5 signers): 31 x 8 x 5 = **1240 takes**. Smoke test (2 signers, 5 takes): 310. Recommended level (8 signers, 10 takes): 2480.
- Optional, to measure the webcam domain shift of the 9 known signs: 5 takes each per signer = 45 more takes per signer.
- Rejected takes do not count; plan for about 20 percent retries (see the training plan).
- Record them in the Record sign tab, Guided session, preset **Conversation vocabulary** (lists only the signs to record).
